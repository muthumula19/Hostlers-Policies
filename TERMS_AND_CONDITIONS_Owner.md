## 6. Booking Management

### 6.1 Booking Approval Process
- Owners must review all booking requests by evaluating the guest's profile, submitted documents, and advance payment status.
- Every booking must be **APPROVED** or **REJECTED** within **48 hours** of submission.
- Rejections are permitted only for the following reasons:
  - Incomplete documentation
  - Non-compliance with hostel or platform policy
  - Property being at full capacity
- When rejecting a booking, owners must clearly state the reason(s) to the guest through the app's communication system.
- **Booking Status Flow:** `PENDING` → `APPROVED` (guest becomes active) | `REJECTED`

### 6.2 Guest Document Verification
- Owners may access guest identification documents strictly during booking and check-in processes.
- **Acceptable verification documents:**
  - Aadhaar Card
  - Passport
  - Driving License
  - Voter ID
  - PAN
  - Utility Bill
  - Bank Statement
- Owners may view these only for the purpose of confirming identity or address during onboarding.
- **Retention after checkout or sharing with third parties is expressly forbidden.**
- Any violation (retaining or sharing documents after guest departure) will result in immediate account termination and may trigger legal liability.

### 6.3 Advance Payment Management
- The advance payment amount is set by the owner and must be collected at the time of booking confirmation.
- Advances are refundable if a booking is cancelled, as per the property's stated cancellation policy.
- Advance amounts may be adjusted against the guest's final bill at checkout.
- Advances are separate from security or damage deposits, which may be required at check-in.

- Transactions without valid evidence (transaction ID or receipt) are not to be accepted as paid.
- **Acceptable Payment Methods:**
  | Method | Requirement |
  |--------|-------------|
  | UPI | Transaction ID required |
  | Bank Transfer | Transaction ID required |
  | Card | Transaction ID required |
  | Cash | Signed/printed receipt must be issued |

### 7.3 Comment System for Rent Communication

### 7.4 Late Payment Management

## 8. Vacate Request & Refund Management

### 8.1 Vacate Request Approval
- Owners must review all guest vacate requests, which include:
  - Intended vacate date
  - Checkout time
  - Reason for leaving
- The notice period for vacating shall be a minimum of **30 days**, or as specified by the hostel policy at the time of booking.
- **Vacate Status Flow:** `PENDING` → `APPROVED` | `REJECTED` → `COMPLETED`
- Approval or rejection must be communicated within **2-3 business days**.
- Reasons for rejection may include:
  - Outstanding bills
  - Insufficient notice period
  - Pending damage assessment
- The owner must provide clear reasons for rejection with an explanation to the guest.

### 8.2 Refund Calculation Responsibilities
- Owners are responsible for verifying that all pending bills are fully cleared prior to processing the refund.
- Damage assessment must be conducted through an inspection (guest participation is encouraged).
- **Refund calculation must include:**
  - **Early checkout penalty:** (Days short of notice × Daily rate)
  - Normal wear and tear are **not chargeable**
  - Damage or other deductions must be itemized and documented
- Guests must be provided with a detailed refund breakdown, including all charges, deductions, and final payable amount.

### 8.3 Notice Period Enforcement
- The standard notice period shall be **30 days** unless otherwise specified.
- Owners must clearly communicate notice requirements at the time of booking.
- Early checkout penalties must be automatically calculated and applied based on the notice period.
- In emergency situations, the owner's discretion can waive notice requirements, but proper documentation must be maintained.

### 8.4 Checkout and Final Settlement
- A room inspection with the guest is recommended at checkout.
- All damages must be documented with:
  - Photos
  - Itemized list
  - Cost estimates
- Final bill shall include all charges, deductions, and refund amount.
- Issue a settlement receipt to the guest.
- Return the damage deposit if there are no damages.
- Close the guest's account after full settlement of dues and refunds.
